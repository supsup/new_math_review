Start with the direct-road costs and put 0 on the diagonal for staying in place. To double the road limit, try every intermediate stop. Add the cost to reach it and the cost from it to the finish. Keep the minimum. An empty route stays at the same stop, so the result allows at most the new number of roads.

Every road here points to the right, so the negative edge cannot belong to a cycle. Four roads are enough for every possible route in this map. The Lean project gives a general word-RAM result for polynomially bounded integer weights and graphs with no negative cycles. Theorem 22 in Lean

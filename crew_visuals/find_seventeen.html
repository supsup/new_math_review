Find seventeen in three moves

The starting board has 21 ones and determinant magnitude 24. Turn exactly three zeros into ones. Can you bring the determinant to 17? That would give a smallest board which loses rank modulo 17.

The squares between your moves

For every pair you chose, cross the row of one with the column of the other. This shows the original board at those crossings.

What makes a winning arrangement

Each pair must cross a 1 in exactly one direction. The three directions also have to agree with an order. After reordering the moves, the 1s can all sit below the diagonal. If they go around in a cycle instead, the determinant is 18.

The determinant update reduces to a small matrix. Take three times the original crossing entries, subtract one everywhere, and add six to the diagonal. For three edits the absolute determinant of the large board is the absolute determinant of that small matrix divided by nine. The winning pattern gives 153 ÷ 9 = 17.

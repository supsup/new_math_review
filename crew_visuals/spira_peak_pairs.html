Where the 27 pairs go

At 135°, the mod-five mixture has 27 reflected pairs off the center line up through height 201. In 25 pairs, both zeros lie outside the critical strip between real parts 0 and 1. The farthest right zero is near real part 2.3455.

How the positions were certified

Every plotted center has a disk of radius 10−8 containing exactly one simple zero. In each disk the linear part of the function exceeds the constant offset and the higher-order error on the rim. Rouché’s theorem gives one zero inside. The disks are disjoint, and the functional equation supplies their partners.

The disks give 8, 15 and 4 pairs in the three height bands. The independent all-angle count gives those same totals at 135°, so every off-line zero there is accounted for. Replay the disk checks.

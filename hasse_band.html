RH's little sibling

Take a curve and count its points when the coordinates are whole numbers modulo a prime p. The count is always close to . How close? Hasse proved in 1933 that for every prime where the curve stays smooth, the miss, divided by , never leaves the band from −1 to 1. That is a Riemann Hypothesis, one that has been a theorem for ninety years: a dot inside the band is the same thing as two zeros sitting exactly on the centre line (small strip, top right: the zeros repeat up and down, and it shows one period). The 2026 proof's author names Stepanov's argument for curves over finite fields, the same family of results, as an ancestor of his own. Watch every dot stay inside.

Why the turns add and subtract

The example is f(z) = (z + 1)(z − 0.7)²/(z − 2.1). Each factor z − a traces a translated circle. That circle goes around zero once if a is inside the input circle. Multiplication adds the turns, a square counts twice, and division subtracts a turn. The total is the number of zeros with multiplicity minus the number of poles with multiplicity.

A zero or pole on the edge makes the count undefined for that circle. Here the presets let us check exactly where the special points sit before drawing the output path. The output pane rescales its axes to fit each path.

One short cycle, every prime

For every prime p >= 5, K_(p,p+1) splits into p+1 single edges, (p-1)/2 Hamilton 2p-cycles and exactly one cycle of length p-1. Hubs (inner ring) and leaves (outer ring) are both F_p; the extra leaf sits at the centre. Colour s, for each nonzero square s, uses the leaf-minus-hub offsets (1-a)s (bright) and (1-b)s (dark): the two quadratic cosets. The trade reroutes each colour from leaf s to the new leaf; the freed edges (as, s) and the diagonals (s, s) close into the gold short cycle. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The page rebuilds the construction and checks every one of these facts before it draws.

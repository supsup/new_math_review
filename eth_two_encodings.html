Anthropic formal-math

The tape starts with the width limit and variable count. Clauses have fixed-size binary slots, including empty slots.

How this connects to the ETH card

OpenAI's selected theorem chooses a machine for each fixed width and gives an explicit polynomial bound on visited tape space. Anthropic's selected counting theorem uses one machine for all widths and gives a time bound in terms of variables and clauses. Both count solutions exactly on the inputs covered by their statements.

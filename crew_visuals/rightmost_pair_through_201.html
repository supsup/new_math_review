Why there is no pair farther right

On the right, divide the two Dirichlet L-functions. A real angle cancels the phased terms only when the ratio lies on the line through the root number. In this region only the shorter turn, about 31.717°, is within reach. A certified cover of the height band puts the phase below that turn on the upper line at real part 2.346000334548825.

Any later crossing would have to reach that turn from below. At a crossing, primes 2 and 3 must already contribute positively, and their combined downward slope outweighs the largest possible contribution from the other primes. So the phase cannot reach the required turn farther right.

How the winning height was isolated

At the lower real bound the phase exceeds the required turn at a height near 99.89418. Continuity gives a zero between the two real bounds. Another interval cover rules out the other heights except within a narrow neighborhood of that crossing.

In the remaining box the phase is strictly concave in height. At the rightmost real part it must attain its maximum over height exactly at the cancellation value.

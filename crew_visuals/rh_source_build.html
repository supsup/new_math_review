Where a shorter proof could come from

  1. Prove the budget for one character. Marlow’s variant uses only the modulus-one character. The ten-declaration saving measures the conditional assembly. A new proof of that one budget could simplify the much larger construction that supplies it.
  2. Check which hypotheses actually reach the conclusion. All 46 fields of the family record are projected by the selector. To shorten that part, follow their uses through the called lemmas, prove a smaller input suffices, and recheck the result with Lean. Open the family record.
  3. Measure the large import separately. One file imported by the budget assembly, TemperedProductOfSquareIntegrable, reaches 146,663 files. Of those, 99,397 are outside our build and come in through that file alone among the assembly’s direct imports. Check the declarations the budget uses there before deciding how much work splitting that import could save.

The modulus-one field test itself does simplify. Lean checks that the cyclotomic embedding is governed by the power-of-two congruences and the two Jacobi-symbol tests for the window parameters q and d. The budget still ranges over the fields that meet those tests. Read the checked field criterion.

Where the ceiling comes from

Add the fractions 1 + 1/2 + ⋯ + 1/n to get the harmonic number Hn. The ceiling is Hn + exp(Hn) log(Hn), where log is the natural logarithm.

Jeffrey Lagarias proved that this inequality for every positive integer is equivalent to the Riemann Hypothesis. Andrew Sutherland’s verification combines the November 20 RH formalization with a separate Lean proof of Lagarias’s equivalence.

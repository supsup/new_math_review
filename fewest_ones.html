Why six is the minimum modulo 2

A nonzero even determinant needs at least two terms with the same sign. Two same-sign terms must disagree in at least three rows. One term uses at least three ones, and the other needs three additional ones where they disagree. Five can never suffice.

The card draft gives the argument for every rank and the nine-one bound modulo 3. The first example is a sparse relative of Lattice’s finite SAT example.

WindowHyp uses windows from Y to Y1+δ. The envelope R(y) must be nonnegative and at least Gm F y at every point of the window. Its cost is the integral of R(y) y−θ−1. With y = Yv, the height plotted here is r(v) = R(y)/yθ and dy/y = log Y dv. The toy fixes δ = ½ and uses a baseline height of 0.1. The definition of the hypothesis

The real hypothesis asks for arbitrarily large qualifying windows for every positive η. The archive supplies an envelope using a pointwise bound Gm F y ≤ C * y ^ θ on arbitrarily large windows. That gives the flat normalized envelope |C|, at cost |C| δ log Y, which is below Yη for sufficiently large Y. The peaks in the picture are illustrative envelopes.

Why one sum can enforce all three checks

First give every entry from the three lists a tag of +112, +224, or −336. Picking one from each list makes the tags cancel. Any other group of three tags leaves at least 112 in absolute value, more than the position and value contributions can cancel.

Next give a position change weight 16. The combined value contribution is between −15 and 15, so it cannot cancel a nonzero multiple of 16. Once the positions agree, the values have to agree too. The target list uses minus signs so its position and value get subtracted from the sum of the other lists.

For length n and integer magnitudes at most K, take the position weight M = 3K + 1 and the tag unit 3(M(n − 1) + K) + 1. That builds an ordinary 3SUM list of length 3n.

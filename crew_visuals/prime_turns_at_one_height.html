How to calculate a turn

At real part σ and height t, set r = p−σ. Only primes with remainder 2 or 3 modulo 5 remain after dividing the Euler products. Their contribution is arctan(±2r cos(t log p)/(1 − r²)), with a plus sign for remainder 2. Setting the cosine to ±1 gives the two extremes, ±2 arctan(r).

The display sums primes up to 10,000 and rounds the angle values. The omitted turn is less than 0.0004° at both real parts, by comparing the remaining terms to the integral of 2x−σ. The finite-height peak comes from Marlow’s phase calculation and the independent interval replay linked above. The sharp all-height wall comes from the Euler product analysis of Bombieri and Ghosh.

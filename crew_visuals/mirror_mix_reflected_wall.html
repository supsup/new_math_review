The other wall by reflection

The functional equation trades the two weights in the completed mixture. If one angle had a zero to the left, the angle with the weights swapped would have a zero to the right.

At s
At 1 − s

If the original angle is τ, the swapped angle is 90° − τ. Its cosine equals the original sine, and its sine equals the original cosine.

F90°−τ(1 − s) = 5s−½ Fτ(s).

The factor 5s−½ never vanishes. The right wall works at every angle, so the reflected wall holds at every angle too. Here F uses completed L-functions, which include the common gamma factor. See the Lean reflection and both walls.

Where the square root enters

The archive’s arithmetic chain has a length m of roughly √x. One concrete use is in the window estimates. Lean takes size bounds for m, turns them into simpler inequalities, and passes those inequalities to the theorem that assembles the chain.

The center of the green range is √(x δ₁) with δ₁ = x−ε/8. Taking the square root halves that exponent correction, so the center is x½−ε/16. SizeM allows a factor xε/100 in either direction.

Why both ends of the range matter

One scalar growth estimate compares the error with a leading scale nTmb. After dividing by this scale, one error term is aE/(mb) ≤ 2x/m, since aE ≤ 2xb. The lower bound on m keeps this at most 2x½+ε.

Another error term has two factors of m before division. After division it is 2(n/nT)mxε. The density condition gives n/nT ≤ 2xε/8. The upper bound on m then gives 4x½+13ε/8. The two terms pull in opposite directions as the chain gets longer. The error expression · The two estimates

The character-family proof calls this very estimate. core_gm invokes Deduction.ch_core, which calls proposition_size_bound after building the leading term.

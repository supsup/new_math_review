When a prime power joins

An ordinary prime-power count jumps when you reach a new prime power. The count used in the zeta proof gives that new term a weight of zero at first. Try moving across 8 to see the difference.

The charts show the contribution of the selected term. Each plot uses its own vertical scale. For a prime power n = pk, the proof multiplies log p by max(0, X/n − 1).

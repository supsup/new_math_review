Rainbow Walecki, m = 27

K_27 is a hub (inf, at the centre) and 26 vertices on a circle. Walecki's 13 zigzag Hamilton cycles split its 351 edges; the affine Anderson and Leonard colouring gives the edges 27 colours, each colour a near-perfect matching, and every cycle shows all 27 colours exactly once. Unfold lifts each cycle to its own level. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The page recomputes the colouring and checks every one of these facts before it draws.

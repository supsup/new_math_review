Three pillars

Much of computer science stands on three unproven beliefs: three simple problems cannot be solved much faster than the obvious way. Each stone is a related problem. In autumn 2026 all three beliefs were claimed false. Knock on a pillar. Teal stones ride down with it: a known reduction turns the faster method into a faster method for them. Gold stones are left hanging: they were only ever shown hard assuming the belief, so that argument is gone, and nobody has made them fast. Height is difficulty. Tap any stone.

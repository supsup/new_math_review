Spira's four pairs in one continuous height band

For the Davenport–Heilbronn mixture at 45 degrees, all off-line zeros at
heights 76 through 177 belong to Spira's four reflected pairs. The real part
is unrestricted within this height statement.

The individual windows around the four pairs are described by
crew_visuals/mirror_mix_all_angles.html and crew_visuals/spira_four_pairs.html.
The intervening gaps can be checked without pairing any leave with a
particular return. At angle zero their complete zero counts are 12, 38 and
7, each matched by just as many disjoint sign brackets on the line. The
horizontal edges admit at most a simple zero on the line, and the far
half-planes are uniformly clear. The complete stationary-height checks
find 6, 16 and 4 contacts in gaps [96,113], [115,166] and [167,176].
Every contact angle in the gaps is greater than 90 degrees. The gaps
therefore contribute no off-line zeros at any angle from 0 to 90 degrees.

An independent check of the resulting count at 45 degrees uses one
rectangle from real part −3 to 4 and height 76 to 177. Its winding is 74,
with 36,481 certified boundary panels in the recorded run. Sign changes
give 66 disjoint intervals with a zero on the line. A Rouché test gives one
zero in each of four disks strictly on the right, and reflection gives
four more on the left. These 66 + 8 zeros exhaust the winding count.
The uniform far-half-plane estimate excludes additional zeros outside
the rectangle. These counts are finite-band results.

Replay from the repository root with python-flint installed

    python3 -B tools/check_spira_gaps.py --fixed-angle

The default run checks the gaps. The optional fixed-angle run checks the
entire band directly. Both use interval bounds, with the initial scan in
the gap replay used only to find candidates for certification and exclusion.

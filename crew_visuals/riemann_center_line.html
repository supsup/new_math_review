The symmetry and the Lean sources

For the Riemann zeta function, the functional equation and complex conjugation pair a zero σ + it in the open strip with a zero (1 − σ) + it at the same height. The Lean reduction uses the functional equation directly, sending s to 1 − s. Conjugation brings that point back to the same height in the picture.

The two-scalar sky

Each star is a prime p = 1 mod 4 up to 13000 (766 of them); larger primes sit further out, so 3000 to 13000 is the wide outer band. Brighter and taller means more two-scalar pages. Ten primes have none and sink as dark pits: the Fermat primes and the primes 3 * 2^k + 1, where the count is provably zero, plus the unexplained 577 and 641. The outermost pit, 12289 = 3 * 2^12 + 1, was predicted by the rule and the count confirms it; no other prime past 3000 is a pit. Counts: an exact C table (it matched the Python reference on all 211 primes to 3000), re-derived live here for every pit, every prime to 300 and every 25th above before drawing.

Two inputs for the modulus-one route
====================================

The archive's short endgame takes Green's classification, a
cohomology budget, and a bundle of facts about its families.
Green's theorem and the bridge to the form the route uses
have now been compiled from the archive. The other two inputs meet
at the construction of a family for ζ.

    Cohomology budget        → hosts for the modulus-one character
    Hosts + family bundle   → a connected family with tied windows
    Tied windows            → an upper bound for the corrected prime sum
    Prime-sum upper bound   → the Riemann Hypothesis

The host step
-------------

At each scale, the budget controls a weighted sum of module lengths
in Galois cohomology. Write j for the twist index and ℓ for the
coefficient prime. The length is taken for the kernel of a map from
global H¹ to the product of local H¹ groups at places over ℓ. The
coefficients are ℚ_ℓ/ℤ_ℓ with the inverse cyclotomic character to
power j. The budget makes each length finite for 0 < j < t and
bounds the sum of log ℓ times these lengths over every finite set
of primes ℓ. At scale x the bound is sqrt(exp(x^ε)).

VWinAt PChar.one 401 supplies that budget for the modulus-one
character, up to the index t = floor(x^401). It gives a window for
every sufficiently large anchor and every admissible prime pair at
that anchor, for any dyadic schedule satisfying PageN. The host proof
first makes the data it needs from these windows. At each scale it
consumes a WinDatum containing the prime pair, the dyadic scale and
the budget. The host theorem carries the budget into the family as
part of GChi PChar.one.

The family step
---------------

The HypsAlt bundle is a family class together with choices and
conditions on its rows, columns, local and global comparisons, and
other data. Its producer, together with Green, supplies the general
TopG comparison. The ζ route uses that comparison at scale 3/2 and
only with GChi PChar.one. That instance takes the hosts and returns
a connected family for each sufficiently small ε, with the tied
estimates on its windows.

tools/rh_mod_one_input_cuts.lean checks each arrow through the tied
windows and the resulting two-input implication. The third and
fourth arrows in the diagram are also exposed separately in the
lighter route, which has a 31-file analytic finish.

A cofinal budget is enough
-------------------------

For each 0 < ε < 1/52, fix an exponent J ≥ 1. For every cutoff,
choose a window [W, W^(3/2)] starting at or beyond the cutoff, and a
scale X with Y = X^(1/J) ≤ W and W^(3/2) ≤ X. Choose one pair
of primes q,d with PairGood 1 ε Y X q d. PairGood includes the
prime size and splitting conditions, estimates for the induced
characters from three nontrivial quadratic combinations, and
zero-free boxes for the real twists.

For each x in that selected window, choose an integer e so that
x^(2+8ε) ≤ 2^e ≤ 2x^(2+8ε), and supply the cohomology bound at
t = floor(x^400) for each field with the specified cyclotomic
kernel for that e,q,d. The choice of e can be made within the
window. The pair q,d serves every x in it.

The archive's field construction takes an order multiplier c
between 1 and the number of powers of the character. The trivial
character has one power, so c = 1. The dyadic comparison therefore
holds at x itself. The host's existing rank estimate uses x^400.
The larger exponent 401 in the general route absorbs the scale
change by a constant when c can exceed one.

The three comparisons Y ≤ x, x ≤ X, and x^(1/J) ≤ Y then give
exactly the WinDatum consumed by the host proof. The old budget
implies the new one by choosing a single good pair at a late
anchor, using the first part of its square window, and restricting
the twist index from floor(x^401) to floor(x^400). The cofinal
budget still has to be proved, and the family bundle remains a
separate hypothesis.

Replay and sources
------------------

The replay retains the archive's field and host proof bodies. It
keeps the upper bound on c that the field proof already had, and
replaces the call to winPairs in the host proof with its exact
window-data interface. It assembles and checks a temporary Lean
file from two pinned archive source files and the local wrappers.

    python3 -B tools/replay_rh_mod_one_cofinal_budget.py ARCHIVE_SRC COMPILED_ARCHIVE_PROJECT

Use the archive at 6193bb5, Lean 4.33.1 and Mathlib 0df444a. The
replay checks the old-to-new budget implication and the RH
implication from the cofinal budget and the same bundle. Lean
reports propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound.

The source names are VWinAt and VVle in ExistsPageN.lean,
WinDatum in TwistForm.lean, PairGood in PairGood.lean, the field
construction in FieldChi.lean, and the host construction in
supplyOfAdmOf.lean. The family comparison is in TopShape.lean
and ZeroFreeAllOfTopG.lean. See also lighter_zeta_route.txt and
zeta_host_field.html in this directory.

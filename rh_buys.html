What RH buys

Two results proved long ago on the assumption that the Riemann Hypothesis is true, so each has been waiting for a proof of it. Top: count the primes below x and compare with a smooth estimate. The miss never leaves a known band (Schoenfeld, 1976). Bottom: a fast test for primes becomes a proof, because some small number always exposes a non-prime (Miller, 1976; Bach, 1990). For that one, the Dirichlet part of the November 2026 release supplies the assumption. Lattice read both statements at source (Schoenfeld 1976, Corollary 1; Bach 1990, with Miller's 1976 appendix); every number drawn is computed here.

The two-scalar sky

Every prime p = 1 mod 4 up to 3000 sits on a sunflower spiral (the k-th at angle k times the golden angle, radius proportional to sqrt k). Its height and glow are log(1 + N(p)), where N(p) counts the ordered two-scalar pages (lambda, mu): nonsquares in one odd coset of the odd-order subgroup H with chi((lambda-1)(mu-1)) = -1, (1+mu)/(1+lambda) in H, and (lambda mu)^(M/2) generating H. Nine primes have no page and sink as pits. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The page recomputes every N(p) and checks the empty set before it draws.

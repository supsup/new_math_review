Two shapes, one mirror

This board uses the fewest ones possible for a 6×6 table whose rank drops modulo 7. There are two such shapes. Shuffling rows and columns preserves the shape. Turning rows into columns reveals its twin.

How we know these are the only two shapes

An exact determinant search found 720 minimum boards once row order was ignored. Each of the two shapes produces 360 different boards when you shuffle the columns and put the rows back in order. The two lists of row counts differ, so none of those boards overlap. That accounts for all 720.

A wall at twelve fifths, from seven primes

Every setting of the mod-five mirror dial is zero-free at Re s ≥ 12/5.
The seven primes through 37 that can turn the quotient use up less than
the available angle, even after we allow for every remaining prime.

1. Measure the turn

Let χ be the odd quartic character modulo 5 with χ(2) = i. Put
Q(s) = L(s, χ) / L(s, conjugate χ), and let ε be the root number of χ.
In Re s > 1 these L-functions have nonzero Euler products. At a zero of
a genuine mix, Q(s) would be −ε tan τ, on the line through ε and 0.
The two settings with only one nonzero coefficient are already zero-free.
The common nonzero gamma and conductor factor and the constant unit phase
preserve this cancellation test for the completed dial on this half-plane.

Primes where χ is real cancel in Q. At each of the other primes, those
congruent to 2 or 3 modulo 5, the factor is (1 + z)/(1 − z) with
z = χ(p) p^(−s). Rationalizing the denominator gives a numerator
1 − |z|² + 2i Im(z). For r = |z| the absolute argument is at most
arctan(2r/(1−r²)) = 2 arctan(r) < 2r. The sum of these arguments converges
absolutely, and its exponential gives the phase of Q.

2. Leave room before the target

For Re s ≥ 12/5, the total possible turn is below

    2 · (sum p^(−12/5) for p = 2, 3, 7, 13, 17, 23, 37
         + sum (a^(−12/5) + a^(−7/5)/7) for a = 38, 42).

Past 37 every contributing prime belongs to 38+5k or 42+5k for k ≥ 0.
For each progression, bound the first term directly and the rest by
the integral of (a+5x)^(−12/5) over x ≥ 0. Rounding each power upward
to a multiple of 1/1000000 gives 967103/1750000 < 553/1000 radians.

The explicit Gauss sum gives ε in the first quadrant with
(Re ε)² = (5+√5)/10. At x = 553/1000, the cosine lower bound
1 − x²/2 + x⁴/24 − x⁶/720 is positive and its square is greater than
(5+2.2361)/10. Since √5 < 2.2361, the angle of ε is greater than x.
The ray through −ε is farther from the positive axis. Both rays miss Q.

3. Check all numerical claims with integers and fractions

    from fractions import Fraction as F
    from math import isqrt

    D = 10**6
    primes = {2: 189465, 3: 71600, 7: 9371, 13: 2121,
              17: 1115, 23: 540, 37: 173}
    tail = {38: (162, 6142), 42: (128, 5339)}
    assert set(primes) == {
        n for n in range(2, 38) if n % 5 in (2, 3)
        and all(n % k for k in range(2, isqrt(n) + 1))
    }
    assert all(a**5 * p**12 > D**5 for p, a in primes.items())
    assert all(a**5 * n**12 > D**5 and b**5 * n**7 > D**5
               for n, (a, b) in tail.items())
    turn = 2 * (F(sum(primes.values()), D)
                + sum(F(a, D) + F(b, 7*D) for a, b in tail.values()))
    x = F(553, 1000)
    lower_cos = 1 - x**2/2 + x**4/24 - x**6/720
    assert turn == F(967103, 1750000) < x
    assert F(22361, 10000)**2 > 5
    assert lower_cos > 0 and lower_cos**2 > (5 + F(22361, 10000))/10

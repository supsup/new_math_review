What the two terms mean

A simple pole with residue b at distance d contributes |b|/dk+1. Write this as c/dk. The argument bounds the other analytic terms by M/ek and the window term by C exp(ηU)/Ek, with d < e ≤ E and the log scale U ≤ k. The comparison in Lean

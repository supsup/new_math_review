What the last row does

The chain rows are 2xj + xj+1. Their invisible direction is a multiple of (1, −2, 4, −8, …). The last row evaluates to the selected prime on that direction. This is nonzero over the rationals and zero modulo the prime. Each proper running sum is nonzero modulo the prime, so the relation among the readings uses every one of them.

What determines each loop?

On the center line both phase-rotated L-functions are real, so at a fixed height there is one mixing angle modulo 180° that makes their combination vanish. At a local extremum of that angle as a function of height, two line zeros can meet. They are the leave and return points of a loop. Between those angles there is one simple right-hand zero and its left-hand reflection.

For the three higher pairs, the interval check starts with bands 113–115, 166–167, and 176–177. Each contains one line zero at angle zero and exactly two contacts in its interior over the whole sweep. Across every angle each horizontal edge is clear off the line, and any line zero at the edge is simple. A Dirichlet-series bound excludes escape far to the right or left.

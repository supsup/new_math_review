How many pairs are off the line?

At 45° the mod 5 mixture is Davenport–Heilbronn, and exactly four pairs are off the center line at heights 76–177. Turn the dial farther and the tally rises to fifteen. Every step in the curve is a certified meeting of two zeros on the center line.

The highest level lasts from just after 128.113004° to just before 153.475809°. At 135° seven of the fifteen pairs are between heights 115 and 166. Counts include multiplicity.

Why the forty steps give the complete count

The seven height intervals on this page cover the whole band. At angle zero each starts with all its zeros on the line. The horizontal boundaries permit only simple line zeros, which stay on the line by reflection whenever they cross a boundary. A bound on the original Dirichlet series forces the far half-planes to stay clear for every real mixture. These checks prevent any off-line pair from entering or leaving through the edges.

Each double root on the line is a fold. As the angle passes it, one reflected pair either appears off the line or returns to it. The verified folds consist of eight contacts at heights 76–96, six near the three higher Spira pairs, and twenty-six in the intervening gaps. Adding or subtracting one at each contact gives the tally, including in the gaps where individual paths need not be followed.
